Not shown, and why

arome_france and arpege_europe are excluded on purpose. Their SP2 t is skin temperature (height: skin in config/models.yaml, corrected T45), not 2 m air temperature — roughly double the diurnal amplitude, so it cannot honestly share this fixed 2 m scale. The Open-Meteo point models (ukmo_global, gem_global, jma_gsm, cma_grapes_global) and aemet_harmonie have no gridded reader in this project and are out of scope for a map.
